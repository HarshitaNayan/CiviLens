CivicLens
Hackathon prototype · not affiliated with any government body

Turn Local Problems Into Action.

CivicLens turns a photo and a short description of a civic issue — a pothole, an overflowing bin, a broken streetlight — into a structured, actionable report in seconds.

Live issue simulation — demo city grid

How it works

From a raw complaint to a report a department can act on, in four steps.

01

Spot it

See a civic issue on your street — take a photo where it stands.

02

Report it

Upload the photo and add a short note. No forms, no jargon.

03

AI structures it

CivicLens detects the issue type, priority, and the department it belongs to.

04

Track it

Every report gets an ID and a place in your dashboard, from submission to resolution.

Why CivicLens

Reporting a civic issue today usually means a phone call, a vague app form, or giving up.

No knowledge of "which department" required

CivicLens reads the issue and routes it to the right one — waste management, roads, electrical, or water.

Reports that read like reports

A one-line complaint becomes a structured description with a category, priority, and a recommended action attached.

Visibility, not a black hole

Every submission gets a tracked ID and status, and feeds a live dashboard of what's happening around the city.

Key features

Built for the reporting flow, end to end.

◈AI issue detection

Analyzes the uploaded photo and description together to classify the issue type and severity.

▤Structured reports

Every submission is turned into a category, priority, department, and recommended action.

✎Editable before submit

Review and correct the AI's read on the issue before it's sent anywhere.

◎Report tracking

A unique ID and status for every report, visible under My Reports.

▥City-wide insights

A live dashboard of issue categories, priority mix, and recent activity.

⬡Works on any device

No install — report an issue from a phone at the location itself.

Future possibilities

What CivicLens grows into beyond this prototype.

Direct department integration

API handoff into real municipal ticketing systems instead of a demo queue.

Duplicate detection

Group repeat reports of the same pothole or bin into one tracked case.

Resolution verification

A follow-up photo request to confirm an issue was actually fixed.

Public heatmaps

Ward-level views so residents and officials see where issues cluster.

This is a hackathon prototype. CivicLens is not officially connected to any municipal or government department. Reports submitted here are stored locally in your browser for demonstration purposes only.
